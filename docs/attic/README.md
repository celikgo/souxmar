# Attic — superseded planning artefacts

The documents in this directory were written during souxmar's design process
as a way of sequencing the work: `SPRINT_PLAN.md` laid out the intended order
of implementation, and `retros/` recorded what each block of work had actually
changed. They are **not** a record of a real team or a real calendar — souxmar
has one contributor, the "sprints" were planning units rather than dates, and
the named roles, velocities and ceremonies in them describe a hypothetical
organisation that never existed.

They are kept because they explain *why* parts of the codebase are shaped the
way they are, and several ADRs cite them. Nothing here is maintained, nothing
here is authoritative, and nothing here appears in the documentation site's
navigation. For what souxmar actually is today, read the
[README](../../README.md); for how it got there, read
[`docs/HISTORY.md`](../HISTORY.md).
